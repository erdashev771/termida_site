What is Termida?

Termida is an AI-powered calorie tracking and nutrition management mobile app, currently in public beta.

Is Termida available for Android and iOS?

Yes. The public beta is distributed as an Android APK and an iOS IPA through MediaFire. Google Play and App Store releases are coming soon.

How can I download the beta?

Choose your platform in the beta download section to open the APK or IPA download page on MediaFire.

Download beta ↓
How do I install the iOS IPA?

Downloading the IPA does not install the app automatically. Installation requires a Windows computer or a separately purchased, compatible iOS signing certificate.

Get IPA installation help on Telegram
When will the app launch in stores?

Google Play and App Store releases are coming soon. A launch date has not been announced on this website.
